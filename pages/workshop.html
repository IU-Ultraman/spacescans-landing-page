ISEE 2026 Workshop

WS06 — SPACESCANS: Tools for Ontology-Driven Spatiotemporal Linkage and Analysis of the Spatial and Contextual Exposome

A half-day pre-conference workshop at ISEE 2026 · Munich, Germany · 30 August – 2 September 2026

Workshop Materials

All attendee materials are in one shared folder — open the Google Drive folder ↗. No sign-in required.

It contains:

1.

The workshop tutorial (PDF)

2.

Two demo datasets of 10,000 simulated participants — one minimal file for the linkage step, one with outcome and covariates for the analysis step

3.

The R scripts for the ExWAS and prediction pipelines, in the RScripts subfolder

What the Session Covers

1.

Background on the spatial and contextual exposome, and an ontology organizing natural, built, and social environmental domains

2.

Live demonstration of the linkage workflow — geographic units and buffers, spatial grids and time windows — followed by hands-on exercises on the demo dataset

3.

Downstream pipelines on the linked output: exposome-wide association studies and predictive modeling, including how to handle high-dimensional, correlated exposures

4.

Questions, discussion, and future directions for spatial and contextual exposome tools and standards

Run It Yourself

The hands-on exercises run in a browser — nothing to install. Open the SPACESCANS web app repository ↗ in GitHub Codespaces and the linkage app, RStudio, and the workshop scripts all start together.

The same R scripts ship in that repository under ISSE26/forAttendee/, alongside the demo cohort.
